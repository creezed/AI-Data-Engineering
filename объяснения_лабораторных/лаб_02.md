```text
  1 | # Сначала считаем платежи и позиции отдельно для каждого заказа.
  2 | sql_task1 = """
  3 | CREATE OR REPLACE TABLE orders_enriched AS
  4 | WITH payments AS (
  5 |     SELECT order_id, SUM(payment_value) AS payment_total,
  6 |            COUNT(*) AS payment_count
  7 |     FROM raw_payments
  8 |     GROUP BY order_id
  9 | ), items AS (
 10 |     SELECT order_id, COUNT(*) AS n_items,
 11 |            SUM(price) AS items_total, SUM(freight_value) AS freight_total
 12 |     FROM raw_items
 13 |     GROUP BY order_id
 14 | )
 15 | SELECT o.order_id, o.customer_id, o.order_status,
 16 |        CAST(o.order_purchase_timestamp AS DATE) AS order_date,
 17 |        COALESCE(p.payment_total, 0) AS payment_total,
 18 |        COALESCE(p.payment_count, 0) AS payment_count,
 19 |        COALESCE(i.n_items, 0) AS n_items,
 20 |        COALESCE(i.items_total, 0) AS items_total,
 21 |        COALESCE(i.freight_total, 0) AS freight_total
 22 | FROM raw_orders o
 23 | LEFT JOIN payments p ON o.order_id = p.order_id
 24 | LEFT JOIN items i ON o.order_id = i.order_id
 25 | """
 26 | 
 27 | con.execute(sql_task1)
 28 | con.execute("SELECT * FROM orders_enriched LIMIT 5").df()
```

- **Строка 1:** Комментарий объясняет, почему платежи и позиции считаются отдельно.
- **Строки 2–3:** Задаёт SQL для создания или замены orders_enriched.
- **Строки 4–8:** CTE payments группирует платежи по order_id, суммирует payment_value и считает операции.
